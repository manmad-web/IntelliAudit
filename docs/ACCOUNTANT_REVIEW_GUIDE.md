# Accountant review guide

The current pilot contains 20 revenue-recognition review cases from five source
companies. The reviewer packet uses pseudonyms so that company reputation does
not supply an answer. These are reconstructed statements and synthetic case
information, not authentic invoices, contracts, audit workpapers or allegations
of errors by the source companies.

## Before you begin

Sign in with the personal code supplied by the study coordinator. Keep it
private. Open **Review guide** for a quick glossary, or **Try a practice case**
for a fictional example outside the pilot. Practice answers are never submitted
to the database and do not count toward progress.

## Reviewing a case

1. Read the accounting context: statement type, US GAAP framework, reporting
   period, currency and unit. Unknown source or date information should remain
   unknown; do not infer it from a familiar company or amount.
2. Read the reported statement. The row labels identify affected lines; the
   reported amount is what you are assessing.
3. Read the supporting information. Component movements are synthetic account
   summaries for reconciliation. Period-end facts are separate supplied
   conditions relevant to recognition. Decide whether these facts are adequate,
   consistent and plausible, and distinguish internal consistency from source
   authenticity.
4. In **Stage A**, choose supported, contradicted, insufficient information or
   unclear. Select affected rows and relevant evidence, describe missing
   information, enter a short reason and choose confidence. Evidence labels
   such as EV-001 are references, not hints about correctness.
5. Flag unclear statements, missing or conflicting facts, unrealistic scenarios
   or source problems. A problem flag is useful feedback, not a failed answer.
6. Expand **Stage B** if you can supply accounting-standard or detailed proof
   information. Unknown/not verified is accepted. A specific citation is not
   required for an initial assessment. Mark a paragraph or dated source as
   verified only after checking it. A sufficient evidence set means that its
   selected items jointly support the conclusion; leave it empty if that is
   unestablished.
7. Submit the initial assessment. The saved record is immutable. Browser drafts
   are separate and become server records only on submission. Continue through
   the queue; the progress notice shows the number of saved initial answers.

## Comparing proposals

The current protocol opens proposal comparison immediately after all 20 initial
assessments have been submitted. The twentieth submission shows its generated
proposal automatically; opening another completed case also shows its proposal.
Compare it with your preserved initial answer and record agreement,
disagreement/revision, unresolved issues or a flag for exclusion, with a reason.
This feedback creates a separate record; it does not replace the first answer.

A generated proposal is a candidate, not verified accounting truth. Accepting
it does not certify its citation, evidence, source authenticity or suitability
for a benchmark release. The current immediate-comparison protocol does not
measure delayed intra-reviewer reliability. Existing packets created under the
earlier protocol retain their scheduled delayed repeat and disclose this in
the workspace.

## Saving and resuming

Your submitted records belong to your account and remain available after signing
in again. Local drafts stay in the same browser. Use **Export my reviews** to
download saved records; browser drafts are not included. If the same case has
already been submitted, the workspace shows the preserved assessment instead
of another initial form.

Contact your study coordinator about access problems, source questions or
incorrectly submitted information. Do not create a new identity to overwrite
your original assessment.

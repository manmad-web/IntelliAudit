# Development review meeting guide

Use the v2 packet to learn whether a reviewer can understand the supplied
evidence, complete the annotation fields, and identify flaws. The software is
ready for this development workflow. Meeting completion does not establish
expert accounting validity or make the packet publication-ready.

## Before the meeting

Create a separate invitation for each participant and keep the curator access
code private. Explain what is recorded, who can access it, and how the team
plans to use the responses. Obtain the participant's agreement to that use.
Keep initial decisions independent of student drafts, model outputs, old keys,
company mappings, and other reviewers' answers.

Confirm the candidate's exact credential. “Level 1” is a self-declared
description until the team identifies the program, completion status,
jurisdiction, and relevant experience. An accounting student or exam-level
candidate is **not automatically a licensed CPA**, and CPA status alone does
not demonstrate familiarity with this particular US-GAAP assertion. Do not
enter “verified CPA,” “expert,” or “qualified expert review completed” without
checking the basis. Record the person's own wording and any verification
separately. A teammate's review is useful feedback; do not count the teammate
as an additional independent accounting expert merely because they reviewed
the dashboard.

If the responses will be collected for university research or publication,
ask the supervisor or responsible research ethics office what process applies
before collecting research-participant data. The relevant institution decides
whether review, exemption, or another process is required. The dashboard does
not grant that approval, infer an exemption, or replace consent and governance.
This is a human process outside the app; an optional record of the decision
can be kept by the team.

## Explain the packet plainly

“You will see twenty related development cases across five company aliases.
The figures are reconstructed from SEC-derived inputs; some rows are computed
residuals. The movements and period-end narratives are simulations, not
authentic invoices, contracts, or a real ledger. Dates distinguish the known
statement period end from an unknown original reporting date. Every monetary
amount is in USD millions. Proposed answers are unvalidated. You may conclude
that evidence is insufficient, reject an assumption, or request a source.
Please explain what information would change your conclusion.”

Use a separate demonstration case to explain navigation and evidence quotes.
Do not use a case in the review inventory to calibrate a desired answer. Check
that the participant understands negative values, component movements,
derived-row flags, and the difference between a supplied fact and a proposed
accounting conclusion.

## Collect the initial pass

Ask the participant to review all twenty cases before revealing any proposal.
Stage A records the judgment, any supported issue and affected rows, evidence
or missing facts, reasoning, and confidence. Standards details can remain
unknown; the participant must not invent a citation to finish an initial case.
Stage B records agreement, disagreement/revision, or an unresolved response
to the proposal, with applicable standards and proof details when available.
A governing citation needs an applicability explanation; merely finding a
taxonomy link or matching the old key is not enough.

When a reasoning claim relies on a supplied line, select its evidence unit and
quote the relevant words exactly. State assumptions and missing facts
separately. If a governing paragraph cannot be checked with an available
permitted source, mark it unresolved rather than filling in a guessed
identifier. Avoid copying unauthorized standards text into a distributable
packet; pointers and the reviewer's explanation are enough for this workflow.

Observe comprehension and workload without steering conclusions. Record
confusing labels, missing evidence, arithmetic/source concerns, and cases the
reviewer considers unrealistic. Changes to the packet after submission need
a separate version and review scope; initial records stay preserved.

## Reconcile immediately after the full pass

V2 allows proposal reconciliation once the complete initial pass is submitted.
It does not require a seven-day wait or delayed repeats. Compare each proposal
with the participant's initial judgment, discuss disagreements, and save a
separate reconciliation decision and explanation. Preserve the initial
response and every revision. A proposed answer can be rejected or left
unresolved. Curator resolution is an operational record, not automatic expert
gold.

Historical v1 delayed-repeat plans and records stay separate. V2 discussion is
not a delayed intra-rater study. Several reviewers can give independent initial
feedback, but summaries from this small related packet remain descriptive.
If the participant has already seen any key, mapping, or related version,
record that exposure and qualify the independence claim.

## Why this pilot uses only income statements

This twenty-case pilot deliberately holds one narrow US-GAAP revenue/cutoff
task constant so the team can check evidence quality, comprehension, and
annotation workload before expanding. The repository also has balance-sheet,
cash-flow, multi-error, and IFRS material; that does not make those cohorts part
of this pilot. The pilot does not support broad conclusions about the whole
audit, every financial statement, or IFRS.

An income-statement assertion may require information from another statement,
notes, a contract, or a transaction record. When that evidence is not supplied,
request the specific missing fact and leave the conclusion or applicability
unresolved as appropriate. Do not infer the answer from the omission. Expansion
should be a separately sourced, versioned, and reviewed packet with the relevant
cross-statement evidence, rather than adding unrelated statements to make the
current packet look broader.

Proposals become available immediately after all twenty initial responses are
submitted and locked. They are not displayed while individual initial cases
are being answered. The reviewer can then agree, disagree and revise, or leave
the proposal unresolved. This immediate comparison does not turn a proposed
label into a valid answer automatically.

## Questions to discuss

- What exactly is your qualification, and which US-GAAP revenue/cutoff work
  have you studied or performed? What should we avoid claiming about this review?
- Are the statement period, units, negative signs, component movements, and
  computed residual flags understandable? Which captions need clarification?
- Do any simulated facts state a conclusion instead of providing evidence?
  Does the neutral revenue component wording still leave a misleading cue?
- Is each judgment actually supported by the supplied evidence? For an
  insufficient or ambiguous case, what specific missing fact would distinguish
  plausible outcomes? Is the curator's withholding proposal defensible?
- Which affected assertion and rows would you record? Could one statement
  contain several assertions requiring separate publication annotations?
- Which authority could govern the assertion, under what entity/framework,
  effective date, scope facts, and exceptions? Which citation sets are valid
  alternatives, and which references are only taxonomy associations?
- What alternative sufficient proof sets or refuting facts exist? Does removing
  a proof unit change the conclusion, or is it redundant?
- Do related company/year amounts or repeated narratives make later answers
  easier to infer? Have you previously seen any source key or version?
- Which cases should be corrected, excluded, or retained only as illustrations?
  Which source accessions and original filing contexts need reconciliation?
- Which parts of the form slow you down or prevent an honest unresolved answer?
  Would the recorded reasoning be interpretable to another reviewer?

## After the meeting

Export the private study backup through the curator workspace. Summarize
usability findings and unresolved accounting/source questions separately.
Record the actual reviewer qualification and any prior exposure. Do not replace
uncertain decisions with the proposal to improve agreement.

The team may call this a development review or usability session. An expert-
reviewed release requires the relevant qualified human review, reconciled
sources and authority, a resolved or prospectively excluded inventory,
appropriate governance, and the separate publication gates. A passing software
test, a submitted form, or curator approval cannot supply those prerequisites.

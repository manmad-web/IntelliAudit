# Research plan and claim boundaries

The next contribution to test is **evidence sufficiency and accounting applicability**,
not a larger synthetic leaderboard. Start with revenue cutoff; expand only after
one accountant can consistently annotate the observable facts and acceptable
answers. Five companies and twenty review items are a feasibility study, not a
representative sample of accounting practice.

## Candidate benchmark design

Separate judgment, localization, governing authority, and evidential support.
Permit multiple acceptable paragraph sets and multiple sufficient proof sets.
Require dated standards applicability, source provenance and explicit ambiguous /
insufficient-evidence dispositions. Later introduce balanced, internally consistent
source documents and typed evidence actions with declared costs. The current
line-deletion pilot does not yet implement or validate this full design.

For a genuine counterfactual pair, change only the causally relevant condition,
keep nuisance presentation balanced, and verify both expected decisions. A model
flipping its answer is insufficient: both pair members must be correct. Freeze
family/event/company grouping so variants cannot cross train/development/test.

## Proposed method after the review gate

Use a cheap model to state the suspected assertion and required evidence, retrieve
source-traceable authority by issue plus framework/date, then check applicability
conditions before emitting a citation. Allow abstention when facts or authority
are missing. Compare against direct prompting, row/concept retrieval and the same
pipeline with the condition gate removed. Add budgeted acquisition only once the
hidden evidence tasks are validated. These are hypotheses, not demonstrated gains.

Use Opus 5.5 as the only frontier family; select cheap/open-weight comparators
through separate provider/schema preflights. Compare matched evidence/tool access.
Keep a closed-book condition distinct from an assisted system. No new paid calls
until the accountant pilot has informed a frozen protocol and cost envelope.
The capstone ledger retains all earlier charges and conservative reservations.

## Evaluation

Report strict citation agreement separately from reviewer-verified applicability,
judgment/type/row/citation joint correctness, clean specificity, unsupported
citation rate, abstention coverage, proof sufficiency and cost per supported
correct decision. Missing and failed calls remain in the intended-case denominator;
conditional-on-valid results report coverage alongside them. Show each company;
five to ten clusters yield limited generalization and uncertain intervals.

No model score, novelty claim, multi-hop fraud claim, or gold-standard status follows
from software checks. AuditFlow already uses graphs/tools/budgets; FinancialAuditBench
already constructs consistent synthetic audit engagements. The candidate distinction
is their carefully controlled combination with evidence sufficiency, acceptable
proof alternatives and dated authority. Maintain a comparison matrix against
primary papers before claiming this combination is new.

report actual expertise, independence, coverage and intra-rater repeat outcomes.
